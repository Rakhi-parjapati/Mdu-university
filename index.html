Message from the Vice Chancellor

Esteemed Members of the M.D.U. Community,

Heartiest Greetings!

It is with immense pride and a great sense of responsibility that I assume the august office of the Vice Chancellor of Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak.

Maharshi Dayanand University stands as a leading institution of Haryana and the nation, renowned for its academic and research excellence, social outreach, sports, literary accomplishments, and cultural achievements.

Together, we shall build upon the existing strong foundation and take our University to new heights of academic excellence.

I look forward to your creative ideas, cooperation, and enthusiastic participation in this journey of the University growth story, with a strong commitment towards social inclusivity, ethical values, constitutional values, and nation-building.
